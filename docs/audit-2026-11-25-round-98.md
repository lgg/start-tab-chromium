# Round 98 — consumed clock alarm retry

Chrome removes a one-shot alarm before invoking its handler. A failed completion transaction could roll back storage but no longer have an alarm to restore, leaving the countdown stuck without an open new tab. Early delivery could likewise leave the timer active without a future alarm. Recheck the exact active token under the data-write lock and rearm at the original deadline or after a 30-second failure backoff. A paused, reset, deleted or completed token is never resurrected. Regression fixtures simulate one-shot consumption, early delivery, a post-write failure, retry and duplicate delivery. Persistent Chrome storage/alarms API failure remains an external limitation.
